2. Charts

Top Clicked Documents

This chart shows the products that received the most clicks from users. It helps us identify which items are the most popular and most favored by the search engine.

Clicks by Result Rank

This chart displays how many clicks each ranking position received. It is useful to check if our ranking algorithm puts the best results at the top since most clicks should be in the first positions.

Top Searched Terms

This chart shows the most frequent individual words written by users in their searches. It helps us understand what kind of products or features users look for the most.

Average Dwell Time by Click Rank

This chart measures the average seconds users stay on a product page depending on its ranking position. It helps verify if the top results are actually relevant and not just clickbait.

Requests by Page

This chart shows the total number of visits for each endpoint on our website. It helps us see how users navigate across the app and how many home page visits turn into searches or product views.
